# PublicDomainMusic

Public-domain sheet music, written in LilyPond, organized by ensemble.

## Contents

- `BrassQuintet/`
  - `BachMagnificat/` — J.S. Bach (d. 1750)
  - `ElgarEnigma/` — Elgar, Enigma Variations (1899; d. 1934)
  - `HandelWaterMusic/` — Handel, Water Music (d. 1759)
  - `MonteverdiVespers/` — Monteverdi, Vespers (d. 1643)
  - `Hymns/`
    - `ForTheBeauty/` — words: Folliott S. Pierpoint, 1864; music: Conrad Kocher, 1838, adapt. William H. Monk, 1861
    - `FromAllThatDwell/` — words: Isaac Watts, 1719; music: Geistliche Kirchengesänge, Cologne, 1623
    - `LordDismissUs/` — words: John Fawcett, 1773; music: John Hughes, 1907 (d. 1932)
  - `octavizer/` — utility LilyPond script
  - `StaffPaper/` — blank staff paper templates

See [CopyrightedMusic](https://github.com/matthewhanna/CopyrightedMusic) (private) for pieces still under copyright.
